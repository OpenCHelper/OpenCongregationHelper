This guide shows how to organize a special activity, for example the distribution of the invitations to the Memorial,
and how to follow it until the end.

## Before the campaign

1. Open **Campaigns → New campaign**.
2. Enter a clear **Name** (e.g. *Memorial invitation campaign*), a **Short Name** and a **Color**.
3. Set **Valid From** and **Valid Until** to the first and last day of the campaign, then click **Create**.

All the users who can log in are notified of the new campaign.

## Assigning the territories

You can link territories to the campaign in two ways:

- from the **Territories** list: filter the **Unassigned** territories, select them and use **Assign Massively**,
  choosing the campaign in the **Campaign** field;
- from the campaign page: open the **Assignments** tab and click **New territory assignment**.

See [Campaigns](../assignments/campaigns) for the details.

## During the campaign

Open the campaign page every few days:

- **Remaining days** tells how much time is left;
- **Completion status** tells which percentage of the territories has been assigned during the campaign, and warns you
  when the work is behind schedule;
- the **Assignment status overview** chart shows how many territories are still to be assigned, in progress and
  completed.

In the **Territories** list, **Group by → Campaign** shows the territories being worked for the campaign.

## At the end of the campaign

1. Open the campaign **Assignments** tab.
2. Select the assignments still in progress and use **Terminate assignments** to return them all at once.

From the end date on the campaign status becomes **Terminated** and territories can no longer be assigned to it.
