The application fills in the **Territory Assignment Record (S-13)** of a service year with the assignments of the
territories, on the official form.

> ℹ️ The record is written on the edition **S-13 1/22** of the form (the code is at the bottom left of the page).

## Uploading the official form

The official form is not included in the application, because it is revised from time to time: a super administrator
downloads the S-13 from **JW Hub**, in the language of the congregation, and uploads it once.

1. Open **Territories** and click **S-13**.
2. In **Official S-13 form**, choose the PDF downloaded from JW Hub.
3. Click **Download**: the form is kept for the next downloads.

To upload a newer edition, use **Replace the official S-13 form** in the same window.

### When the form has changed

When the form you upload is different from the one the application was made for (e.g. a new edition), a warning lists
the differences before it is used, because the record could be written in the wrong places. Click
**Report it on GitHub**: it opens an issue with the details already filled in. Attach the form to the issue, so that
the application can be updated. To use the form anyway, tick **Use this form anyway**.

## Downloading the record

1. Open **Territories** and click **S-13**.
2. Choose the **Service year**: it runs from September 1 to August 31.
3. Click **Download**.

Each territory takes one row, sorted by name, with the date on which it was last completed before the service year and
the assignments of the service year: who it was assigned to, when, and when it was completed. A territory assigned more
than four times continues on the next row.
